Realistic Patient Simulation through Controlled Diversity and Selective Disclosure

Patient simulators don’t act like humans… until now.

Framework

Each patient is parametrized over the six HEXACO personality axes. A latent patient state decides how the patient talks and what it may reveal at each turn.

Results

Evaluated on 30 real primary-care encounters from the US Department of Veterans Affairs; nine clinicians rated 540 blinded conversations.

Judged realistic by clinicians

PWP is judged realistic more often than the recorded encounters and prior simulators, and almost never flagged as too informative.

75%PWP
47%real encounters
≤30%prior simulators

Real disclosure pacing

PWP follows how real patients reveal information over the conversation; prior simulators front-load the case.

Recoverable, diverse personalities

Clinicians and LLM judges recover the configured traits, and PWP personas span a wider behavioral range than PatientSim on 25 of 30 cases.

Realistic benchmark difficulty

On a contextual-care benchmark, PWP comes closest to the error rate of the real visit; prior simulators volunteer the answer and make the task too easy.
